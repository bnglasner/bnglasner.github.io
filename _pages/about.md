# Benjamin Glasner, Ph.D.

Benjamin Glasner is a Senior Economist at the Economic Innovation Group based in Washington, D.C. His work focuses on labor markets, place-based policy, and social-policy design, with an emphasis on worker well-being and economic mobility.

## Professional Identity

- Current role: Senior Economist, Economic Innovation Group (EIG)
- Research focus: Labor markets; place-based policy; social-policy design and economic well-being.
- Core method: applied microeconomics
- Core method: econometric policy evaluation
- Core method: large-scale data analysis
- Core method: policy communication
