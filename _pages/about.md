# Benjamin (Ben) Glasner, Ph.D.

Ben Glasner is a Senior Economist, Economic Innovation Group (EIG) based in Washington, D.C. His work focuses on labor markets, place-based policy, and social-policy design, with an emphasis on worker well-being and economic mobility.

## Professional Identity

- Current role: Senior Economist, Economic Innovation Group (EIG)
- Core methods: applied microeconomics, econometric policy evaluation, large-scale data analysis, and policy communication
- Research focus: Labor markets; place-based policy; social-policy design and economic well-being.
