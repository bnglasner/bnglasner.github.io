<div class="policy-section" id="long-bio">
  <h2>Long bio</h2>
  <p>
    Benjamin Glasner is a Senior Economist at the Economic Innovation Group and an affiliate of the Center on Poverty and
    Social Policy at Columbia University. He studies U.S. labor markets, place-based policy, and the design of social
    programs. At EIG, he co-authored the 80-80 wage subsidy proposal with Adam Ozimek, led EIG's analysis of how
    Opportunity Zones affected housing supply, and documents gaps in U.S. retirement-plan coverage.
  </p>
  <p>
    His peer-reviewed research on the minimum wage and gig work and on the expanded Child Tax Credit appears in the
    <em>Journal of Labor Economics</em>, the <em>Journal of Public Economics</em>, and <em>Health Affairs</em>. He has
    appeared on C-SPAN's Washington Journal and CBS News, and his research has been covered by The Wall Street Journal,
    The Washington Post, and Reuters.
  </p>
  <p>
    Before joining EIG in 2023, he was a postdoctoral research scientist at Columbia's Center on Poverty and Social
    Policy. He holds a Ph.D. in public policy and management from the University of Washington's Evans School of Public
    Policy and Governance and a B.A. in economics from Vassar College.
  </p>
</div>
