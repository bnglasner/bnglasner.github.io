## Wages, Work & Labor-Market Institutions

How labor-market rules and wage policy shape the well-being of workers—especially those at the bottom of the earnings distribution. This line of work ranges from a comprehensive wage-subsidy proposal to empirical studies of minimum-wage spillovers into the gig economy and the effects of noncompete reform on business formation.

- **[How to end low-wage work forever: An 80-80 wage subsidy proposal](https://eig.org/how-to-end-low-wage-work-forever/)** (EIG, 2025) — A wage-subsidy proposal aimed at raising take-home pay without discouraging employment.
- **[The American Worker: Toward a New Consensus](https://eig.org/american-worker/)** (EIG, 2024) — Mapping the state of the American worker and building a case for new labor-market policy.
- **[The Minimum Wage, Self-Employment, and the Online Gig Economy](https://doi.org/10.1086/719690)** (*Journal of Labor Economics*, 2023) — Evidence that minimum-wage increases push workers toward self-employment and online gig platforms.
- **[The Effects of Noncompete Agreement Reforms on Business Formation](https://eig.org/noncompetes-research-note/)** (EIG, 2023) — Comparing Hawaii and Oregon reforms to understand how noncompete bans affect entrepreneurship.

## Place-Based Policy & Economic Geography

Evaluating place-based tools—especially Opportunity Zones—and documenting the geographic transformation of income sources across American communities.

- **[The Impact of Opportunity Zones on Housing Supply](https://eig.org/opportunity-zones-housing-supply/)** (EIG, 2025) — New evidence on how OZ designation affects housing stock. \[[Code](https://github.com/EIG-Research/oz-housing-supply)\]
- **[The Great "Transfer"-mation](https://eig.org/great-transfermation/)** (EIG, 2024) — Analysis of how many U.S. communities became more reliant on government-transfer income. \[[Code](https://github.com/EIG-Research/EIG-Great-Transfer-Mation)\]
- **[Are Opportunity Zones Working? What the Literature Tells Us](https://eig.org/are-opportunity-zones-working/)** (EIG, 2023) — A survey of the empirical literature on OZ outcomes.

## Transfers, Safety Net & Poverty

Research on how safety-net programs—particularly the Child Tax Credit and food assistance—affect employment, health, and intergenerational poverty.

- **[Effects of the expanded Child Tax Credit on employment outcomes](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpubeco.2024.105168)** (*Journal of Public Economics*, 2024) — Examining whether the 2021 CTC expansion reduced parental employment.
- **[No Evidence the Child Tax Credit Expansion Had an Effect on the Well-Being and Mental Health of Parents](https://doi.org/10.1377/hlthaff.2022.00730)** (*Health Affairs*, 2022) — Testing for mental-health effects of the expanded CTC.
- **[The Effectiveness of the Food Stamp Program at Reducing Racial Differences in the Intergenerational Persistence of Poverty](https://equitablegrowth.org/working-papers/the-effectiveness-of-the-food-stamp-program-at-reducing-differences-in-the-intergenerational-persistence-of-poverty/)** (Equitable Growth, 2023) — Whether early-life food assistance breaks cycles of poverty.
- **[Liquid Assets, Financial Shocks, and Entrances into Material Hardship](https://robinhood.org/reports/annual-report-the-state-of-poverty-and-disadvantage-in-new-york-city-vol-6/)** (Robin Hood, 2023) — How liquid-asset buffers protect families from financial shocks.
