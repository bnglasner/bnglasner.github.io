The Effects of Noncompete Agreement Reforms on Business Formation: A Comparison of Hawaii and Oregon

Benjamin Glasner

Economic Innovation Group

Summary

This research note compares two state-level noncompete reforms: Hawaii’s exemption for technology-sector workers and Oregon’s reform covering lower-wage workers. Hawaii’s targeted tech-sector exemption is associated with a 10 percent increase in the number of technology establishments, while Oregon’s broader low-wage reform shows no statistically significant effect on business formation. The contrast suggests that restricting noncompetes spurs entrepreneurship most effectively when it covers higher-skilled, knowledge-based workers.
