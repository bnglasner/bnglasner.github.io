Evidence on how policy reshapes work, place, and mobility.

I study how policy changes work, pay, and opportunity across U.S. places — from minimum wages and the gig economy to Opportunity Zones, the Child Tax Credit, and retirement coverage. The through-line is how national policy choices play out in local economies, and what that means for worker well-being and economic mobility.
