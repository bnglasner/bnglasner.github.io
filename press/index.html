Long bio

Benjamin Glasner is a Senior Economist at the Economic Innovation Group and an affiliate of the Center on Poverty and Social Policy at Columbia University. He studies U.S. labor markets, place-based policy, and the design of social programs. At EIG, he co-authored the 80-80 wage subsidy proposal with Adam Ozimek, led EIG's analysis of how Opportunity Zones affected housing supply, and documents gaps in U.S. retirement-plan coverage.

His peer-reviewed research on the minimum wage and gig work and on the expanded Child Tax Credit appears in the Journal of Labor Economics, the Journal of Public Economics, and Health Affairs. He has appeared on C-SPAN's Washington Journal and CBS News, and his research has been covered by The Wall Street Journal, The Washington Post, and Reuters.

Before joining EIG in 2023, he was a postdoctoral research scientist at Columbia's Center on Poverty and Social Policy. He holds a Ph.D. in public policy and management from the University of Washington's Evans School of Public Policy and Governance and a B.A. in economics from Vassar College.
